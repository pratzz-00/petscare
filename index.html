Who We Are

Your Pet's Health
Is Our Sacred Mission

At Pet's Care Service, we believe the bond between humans and their animals is one of life's most precious gifts. We honour that bond with clinical excellence and boundless compassion.

Our team of licensed veterinarians and dedicated pet-care professionals brings decades of combined experience — treating every animal, whether a pampered companion or a street dog in need, with the same unwavering care.

What We Offer

Comprehensive Pet Care Services

From routine wellness visits to emergency surgeries — every service delivered with precision and warmth.

Pet Care Service

Dedicated day care and daily health monitoring in a safe, nurturing clinical environment where every pet is treated like family.

Doctor Consultation

One-on-one sessions with licensed veterinarians — thorough examinations, accurate diagnoses, and compassionate guidance for pet parents.

Vet & Doctor Home Visit

No travel stress. Our experienced vets arrive at your home, equipped for examinations, treatments, and minor procedures.

Lab Tests (Blood, X-Ray)

Full in-house diagnostic laboratory with blood panels, X-ray imaging, and rapid results for accurate, informed treatment decisions.

Vaccination & Deworming

Comprehensive preventive care with scheduled vaccinations and deworming protocols tailored to your pet's age, breed, and lifestyle.

Pet Grooming

Professional grooming by trained specialists — bathing, trimming, nail care, ear cleaning, and coat treatments for a happy, healthy pet.

Surgery Facility

State-of-the-art surgical suite for routine to complex procedures, with full anaesthesia monitoring and dedicated post-operative recovery care.

24/7 Ambulance Service

Round-the-clock emergency pet ambulance. Rapid response, equipped vehicles, and trained emergency staff — always ready when every second counts.
